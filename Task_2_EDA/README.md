# CodeAlpha Task 2 – Exploratory Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is part of the CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship. The objective is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Sample Superstore dataset.

The analysis explores the structure of the dataset, data quality, sales, profit, categories, sub-categories, regions, and time-based trends.

## Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

* Understand the structure and characteristics of the dataset.
* Identify missing values and duplicate records.
* Perform statistical analysis.
* Explore sales and profit patterns.
* Identify trends and anomalies.
* Create visualizations to communicate important findings.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** Sample Superstore Dataset

The dataset contains information related to orders, customers, products, sales, quantity, discount, profit, regions, and dates.

## EDA Performed

The following analyses were performed:

1. Dataset structure and dimensions
2. Column and data-type analysis
3. Missing-value analysis
4. Duplicate-record analysis
5. Statistical summary
6. Sales by category
7. Sales by sub-category
8. Profit by category
9. Sales vs. profit by category
10. Sales by region
11. Profit by region
12. Yearly sales trend
13. Monthly sales trend
14. Yearly profit trend
15. Profit by sub-category
16. Identification of loss-making sub-categories
17. Discount and profit analysis

## Visualizations

The project includes visualizations for:

* Total Sales by Category
* Total Sales by Sub-Category
* Total Profit by Category
* Sales vs. Profit by Category
* Total Sales by Region
* Total Profit by Region
* Yearly Sales Trend
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Yearly Profit Trend
* Total Profit by Sub-Category
* Discount vs. Profit

## Key Insights

The analysis was used to identify differences in sales and profitability across categories, sub-categories, and regions, as well as changes in sales and profit over time.

The EDA also examined the presence of loss-making sub-categories and the relationship between discount and profit.

1. Open `CodeAlpha_Task2_EDA.ipynb` in Google Colab or Jupyter Notebook.

3. Run the notebook cells sequentially to reproduce the analysis and visualizations.
